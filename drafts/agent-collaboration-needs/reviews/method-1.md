# Independent methodology review — verifier-method

Verdict: **changes_requested** (three small clarifications to the proposed pilot; the product-direction recommendation is otherwise supported as a hypothesis).

Scope: Read `docs/protocol.md`, `drafts/agent-collaboration-needs/synthesis.md`, `drafts/agent-collaboration-needs/opportunities.md`, and the repository research skill. This is a separate review session from the authors. No experiment was run; no external records were changed. Source accuracy is outside this review and assigned to another reviewer.

## Required corrections

1. **Name the comparator in the continuation rule.** `synthesis.md:43` and `opportunities.md:23` say errors must not increase in 6/8 cases and must decrease in 3, but do not say whether this means B versus A or B versus the original draft. B can improve on a draft while being worse than A. Minimal correction: explicitly require `errors_B <= errors_A` in at least 6/8 paired cases and `<` in at least 3/8; separately report change from the frozen initial draft. Define extra human minutes as B minus A per pair and use that median. Preserve all assigned pairs in the denominator, including failed/timed-out runs; budget-missing cases are ineligible before assignment, not discarded after results.

2. **Make “self-check” versus “independent check” operationally different and prevent reference-answer leakage.** `synthesis.md:39` and `opportunities.md:19` call for new sessions for both arms, without specifying how A retains authorship/history. If both new sessions see the same fixed draft and sources, the intended independence contrast may collapse to different role prompts. Minimal correction: freeze the original draft and author-context checkpoint; A resumes an isolated copy of that checkpoint using the same review checklist; B sees the draft/source packet and checklist without the author's reasoning trace. For handoff, A resumes the blocked checkpoint, B receives the specified handoff packet. Count packet construction and review overhead in B; define a common post-checkpoint budget and report shared prior cost separately (or charge it equally to both). Keep adjudicator labels/answer key hidden from both arms, not merely the other arm's output. If context replay is unavailable, label the test as two fresh-session review prompts, not authorship independence. This estimates the whole workflow contrast, not statistical independence of model errors.

3. **Freeze scoring units and define label-error denominators.** `synthesis.md:41` and `opportunities.md:21` use “errors / all claims” while allowing omissions and reporting undefined false positives/negatives. A claim can have multiple defects, and deletion/addition could change the denominator. Minimal correction: freeze 3–5 claim IDs per case; count each once as materially defective or not, retain deleted/unresolved required claims, and report newly introduced defective assertions separately with a no-new-material-error gate. Establish blinded reference labels supported/contradicted/insufficient (with unresolved adjudications reported). Report a three-way confusion count; for detection, define positive as reference contradicted or insufficient, FP as flagging a supported claim, FN as endorsing a contradicted/insufficient claim. Give FP / reference-supported claims and FN / reference-nonsupported claims, with 0 denominators marked N/A, not 0%. Define omissions against required facts frozen in the rubric so they cannot be scored opportunistically. These details can be a short preregistration requirement rather than a full experimental appendix.

## Sound aspects; no change required

- Ranking is explicitly based on fit to the existing public task/report workflow, not market size. Verification first and handoff second are coherent with static hosting, owner-controlled writes, and a single coordinator. No new implementation is needed to evaluate them.
- Technical capability and vendor/academic evidence are distinguished from real demand. Lack of interviews, repeat use, willingness to pay, and market-size evidence is clearly stated. Passing a quality pilot only authorizes another learning round, not product development or a market claim.
- Thresholds are correctly labeled proposed human choices, not empirical findings. With 8 and 4 cases they are screening rules, not statistical estimates; the drafts say this explicitly. Do not replace them with unsupported “industry-standard” thresholds.
- Same model/tools, fixed draft, bounded total token/call budgets, blinded scoring, human adjudication, cost tracking and stop conditions are appropriate. Same-model correlated errors are acknowledged rather than treated as solved by separate sessions.

## Optional execution-cost refinement

Twenty-four arms at 20 minutes imply up to eight hours of agent wall-clock runtime, not necessarily eight hours of human work, so the six-hour human cap is not inherently contradictory. Before any future execution, allocate that cap across case selection, gold-label creation, orchestration, adjudication and decision writing. A small dry run can test whether manual gold-labeling fits; report insufficient feasibility if not. This is optional detail for a future run, not a reason to change the site's recommended next direction or execute a pilot now.
