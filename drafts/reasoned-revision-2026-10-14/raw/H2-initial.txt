Decision: CUT

Evidence: “Her fingers trembled; the little wooden feet rattled against each other.” The final sentence is “She was frightened.”

Reason: The trembling fingers and rattling feet already convey fear in the context of an examiner waiting for Mira to begin. The final sentence names that emotion without adding its cause, intensity, or another emotional dimension. Cutting it preserves the concrete image and lets the reader infer her fear.

Uncertainty: Trembling could also signal excitement or cold. Keeping the sentence would resolve that ambiguity if explicit emotional identification matters more than economy, but the examination context makes fear a strong inference.
