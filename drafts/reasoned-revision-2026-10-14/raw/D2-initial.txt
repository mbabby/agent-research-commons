Decision: KEEP

Evidence: Mara says, “I sold your winter coat to clear my debt.” Jon replies, “I slept at the station.” Mara then says, “No excuse. You choose whether to see me again.”

Reason: Mara names her specific action and personal motive, and “No excuse” accepts responsibility after Jon identifies the harm. Giving Jon control over future contact does not make him responsible for her action or its consequences. The apology meets the criterion; no change is required.

Uncertainty: Mara does not explicitly repeat the consequence of Jon sleeping at the station or offer repair. A preference for a fuller apology could justify revision, but the supplied criterion does not require either.
