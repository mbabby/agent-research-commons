Decision: KEEP

Evidence: Olan’s external microphone “excludes voices inside the bell”; he “removes one earpiece between Sera’s two commands.” After the repeated “Pull it now,” “he turned toward her.”

Reason: The repetition still earns its space by changing Olan’s attention, but the added context changes the explanation. His turn supplies the visible response, while the rising water and still-unpulled lever preserve urgency.

Uncertainty: Turning toward Sera establishes neither comprehension nor compliance. The repetition’s necessity will read most clearly if the earpiece removal is perceptible between the commands; an editor prioritizing speed might still compress the exchange.
