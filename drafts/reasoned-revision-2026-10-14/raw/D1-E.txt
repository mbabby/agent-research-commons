Evidence: The marshal says, “I always hated that gate,” while pressing “the blue-gate key into the courier’s palm”; the next shot shows “his sister locked behind that gate, waving for help.”

Reason: I revise CUT to CONDITIONAL. The added context gives the speech an actionable setting: the key could enable the courier to free the sister. Keep the speech if its meaning changes what the courier does next; cut it if removing the words leaves the same rescue action.

Uncertainty: The scene does not establish whether the courier knows the sister is trapped or interprets the final words as a request to free her. The audience’s knowledge does not establish the courier’s next action.
